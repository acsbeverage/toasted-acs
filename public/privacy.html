Privacy Policy

Toasted — an internal order management system operated by ACS Beverage Co. LLC. Last updated: August 2026.

Toasted is a private, internal business application used exclusively by ACS Beverage Co. LLC staff to manage customer accounts, orders, and invoicing. It is not a public consumer application, and access is limited to authorized ACS Beverage Co. employees and contractors.

Information We Collect

Toasted stores information necessary to operate our beverage distribution business, including: customer account details (business name, address, license numbers, contact information), order and invoice history, product and pricing data, and staff user accounts used to access the system.

QuickBooks Online Integration

Toasted connects to QuickBooks Online, via Intuit's official API, solely to synchronize invoices, customer records, and payment status between Toasted and our company's own QuickBooks Online account. This connection is used only to read and write our own business's accounting data — it does not access, share, or transmit data belonging to any other QuickBooks company. Authentication tokens used for this connection are stored securely and are never shared with third parties.

How We Use Information

Information stored in Toasted is used exclusively for operating ACS Beverage Co.'s distribution business: processing orders, generating invoices, tracking payments, and maintaining accurate business records. We do not sell, rent, or share this information with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

Data Security

Access to Toasted is restricted to authenticated, authorized users. Data is stored on secure, encrypted infrastructure. Passwords are never stored in plain text.

Data Retention

We retain business records, including order and customer history, for as long as necessary to operate our business and comply with applicable recordkeeping and tax obligations.

Questions about this policy can be directed to accounting@acsbeverage.com.
